Getting there

Marnoo Cricket Ground

Put the full address into your maps app, not just the word Marnoo — and load your directions before you leave town.

Give yourself plenty of time to get there, and arrive early for the best seat.

Parking

  • Park on the streets around Marnoo, not at the reserve — it’s well signposted.
  • Elderly or less mobile? Drop them at the gate first, then park.
  • Follow the parking attendants, and don’t block anyone in.
  • The ground is soft and wet in places — take it steady, and follow the attendants around boggy patches.
  • Short walk in from most streets; a shuttle bus is available if you need it.

Dress for cold

Dry and clear — about 17 degrees in the afternoon, down towards 5 overnight. Outdoors, in a marquee, on a cricket ground.

  • A proper coat and layers
  • Practical shoes — the ground is grass and uneven
  • A torch, or a charged phone, for the walk back to the car

Nobody is dressing up. Come as you would to a footy final in August.

Food, drink and the kids

  • Supper is included in your ticket, cooked by locals.
  • The bar runs all night — local brews and other drinks to buy.
  • No BYO. Licensed event; all drinks stay in the marquee.
  • Kids welcome all night; under 18s stay with a responsible adult.

Staying the night

  • Not driving home? Sort a bed today.
  • Stawell, St Arnaud, Horsham, Donald, Murtoa and Rupanyup all have rooms within an hour.
  • They will be booked out by the weekend.

Getting home

  • Licensed event, three hours from Melbourne, no taxis and no rideshare — plan before you arrive.
  • Nominate a driver or book a bed. Police are aware of the event and there will be booze buses in the district.
  • Kangaroos are out in numbers after dark — and fatigue is real after a big night. Take it slowly.

At the gate

  • Have your ticket ready to scan — one scan per ticket.
  • Bought for a group? Whoever holds the tickets walks in with the group.
  • Qualified first aiders are on site.
  • Stay out of fenced or marked-off areas — it’s a working recreation reserve.
  • There will be photographers and media on the night.

If you need something

  • Tickets and anything else: support@farmersfightback.com
  • On the night: James on 0451 861 705

That is everything. Rug up, leave a bit early, and we will see you at the gate. This is the biggest night this campaign has put on, and it is going to be a cracker.
